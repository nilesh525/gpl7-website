About the League

A festival week, a cricket season

GPL-7 brings Gauripur's cricketers together right through Durga Puja — six teams, an open auction, and a season that runs from Maha Saptami into the final.

6

Teams

Roughly 12 players each, about 72 players total.

15

League Matches

Round robin — every team plays every other team once.

4

Semi-Finalists

Top 4 on points advance to the knockout stage.

Player Registration

Registration opens 6 September. Register and pay first — teams are assigned later through the auction.

  1. Fill in your name, mobile number, and playing role (Batsman / Bowler / All-rounder / Wicket-keeper).
  2. Pay the ₹200 registration fee via UPI.
  3. Upload your payment screenshot and UTR / transaction ID in the form.
  4. Your registration stays PENDING until the committee verifies the payment against the actual transaction.

Rules

Playing conditions

  • Only a legal bowling action is allowed — throw-arm bowling is strictly prohibited.
  • LBW is not applicable. All other standard ICC rules apply.
  • Umpire decisions are final and binding.
  • Misbehaviour with an umpire, the committee, or fellow participants can lead to immediate termination and a ban from future tournaments.
